How it works

Five steps from application to your asset or funds.

  1. Apply online
    Choose a loan, fill in the form and upload your National ID.
  2. Pay the application fee
    Pay securely with MTN, Airtel or card.
  3. We review and call you
    Download your loan pack while our team reviews your application.
  4. Pay your deposit
    Once approved, we email you and you pay your deposit online.
  5. Sign and receive
    Upload your signed documents. Then we release your asset or funds.

Common questions

What do I need to apply?

Your National ID, a phone number and email, details of your work and income, and a guarantor or referee. Each loan page lists what that loan needs.

What do I pay first?

The application fee, which is shown on every loan. You pay it online when you apply. The deposit comes later.

When do I pay the deposit?

Only after your application is approved. We email you, and you pay it from the My application page.

How do I check my application?

Open My application, enter your reference and the phone or email you applied with. You can check your status any time.
